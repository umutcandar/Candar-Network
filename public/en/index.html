What is Candar Network?

Candar Network is a mobile community app that brings together daily streaks, in-app points, missions, referral teams, and Candar AI.

Can I start for free?

Yes. The app is free to download. It contains ads; Premium and boost purchases are optional.

Are Candar points money or cryptocurrency?

No. Candar points are only for in-app progress and features. They do not provide cash or investment returns.

Do I need an invite code?

No. You can create an account without a code. If a friend invited you, add their code during registration.
