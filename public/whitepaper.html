OVERVIEW

A network that grows through daily participation.

Candar turns a simple daily check-in into visible progress: points, streaks, referrals, boost options, leaderboards, and Candar AI support in one app.

The product story is simple: join early, keep showing up, grow your circle, and be ready as the next Candar community experiences open.

Today: collect points. Next: unlock more in-app status and community utility. No financial promises, no investment claims, just product progress and early community positioning.

05

FAIR USE

Growth must stay real.

  • Only real users and real referrals should count.
  • In-app points represent app progress, not a cash balance.
  • Spam, fake accounts, and manipulation can reduce trust and visibility.
  • Future features will be shaped by product quality, retention, and platform policy.
Transparency note

Candar points are in-app points. Candar Network does not promise income, profit, returns, or external monetary value. This roadmap describes planned product direction and may change as the community, app quality, and platform rules evolve.
