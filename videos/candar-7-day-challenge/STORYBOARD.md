## Video direction

- Palette: alternate Broadside’s two registers only — ink-black with white type and fire-orange/gold accents, or a full fire-orange field with ink-black type. No gradients and no second accent.
- Type: one dominant lowercase display statement per frame; tracked mono labels are supporting chrome only. All load-bearing copy stays inside the top 83% Reels-safe region.
- Reveal model: every copy fragment and product proof arrives on its own silent visual cue across the full shot, with smooth long-tail settles. Nothing is dumped into the first quarter.
- Rhythm: Frame 1 is fast and declarative; Frame 2 slows for product proof; Frame 3 regains momentum through mirrored entry; Frame 4 resolves into the longest still read.
- Hold discipline: Frame 2’s final product view and Frame 4’s final URL are deliberate breathers. Holds are static except for a bounded logo jitter or one progress shimmer.
- Negative list: no crypto, mining, price, investment, cash, token, or speculative language; no Points Use Center; no browser chrome; no tiny floating cards; no bouncy default entrances; no lazy breathing, back-half camera drift, slideshow front-loading, or screensaver-style independent motion.
