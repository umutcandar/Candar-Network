CANDAR ROADMAP

Collect today. Be early tomorrow.

Candar'ın en güçlü hissi erken katılım: puanını biriktir, serini koru, ağını büyüt ve sonraki topluluk fazlarına hazır kullanıcılar arasında yerini al.

  1. 01

    First community layer

    Daily points, streaks, referrals, and early user profiles become the base of the network.

  2. 02

    Status and visibility

    Badges, leaderboards, and milestones make consistent participation more visible.

  3. 03

    Utility expansion

    More in-app experiences, partner-ready surfaces, and community missions as usage grows.

  4. 04

    Ecosystem access

    Future experiences shaped by product readiness, community feedback, and platform rules.

Candar points are in-app points. Early participation can unlock stronger in-app positioning, but this is not a financial promise, investment product, or guarantee of external value.
