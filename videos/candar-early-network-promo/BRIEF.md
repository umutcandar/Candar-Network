## Intent

Create an English vertical social promo that makes early participation feel
valuable without promising money, ownership, investment returns, or a future
cash value. The viewer should want to check in daily, collect Candar Points,
grow the referral network, and take a visible place in the ecosystem.

## Assets

- assets/daily.png — real daily reward and streak screen.
- assets/network.png — real referral network screen.
- assets/logo.png — official Candar mark.

## Customizations

- Open with a direct early-participation hook.
- Animate Candar Points as app progress, never as currency.
- End with one download action for iOS and Android.

## Notes

- Do not promise cash, profit, investment value, ownership, or guaranteed future rewards.
- Use “in-app points” and “growing ecosystem” language.
- No paid promotion.
